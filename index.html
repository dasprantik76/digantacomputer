About Diganta Computer Centre

Quality technical education, practical skills, and trusted training since 2005.

Diganta Computer Centre (DCC) is an ISO 9001:2015 certified organization and a registered computer training institute recognized by MSME and Paramount Quality Certifications, London. The institute is registered under the West Bengal Societies Registration Act XXVI of 1961 (Registration No. S/1L/30592 of 2005).

About us

Diganta Computer Centre™

Established with the vision of making quality technical education accessible to everyone, we provide affordable, practical, and career-oriented computer education for students from both urban and rural areas. Our learning environment combines experienced guidance with hands-on training, helping students develop useful digital skills and the confidence to apply them in education, employment, and everyday life.
